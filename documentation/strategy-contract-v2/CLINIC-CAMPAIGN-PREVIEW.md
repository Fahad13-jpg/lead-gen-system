# Selected campaign: Islamabad dental clinic websites

This is an assistant-selected draft, not an approved campaign or a live strategy plan. The accompanying JSON uses the existing campaign configuration format inside `campaign_request`; its surrounding metadata must not be discarded during review.

The proposed offer is a clinic website presenting services and contact details, with an enquiry form. These describe a proposed project, not features of an existing product. No demo, portfolio, customer results, pricing or delivery date has been invented. “Fahad Web Studio” is a proposed sender brand.

Start with email to Islamabad dental clinics. Use the existing website-presence qualifier to prioritize relevant leads, but never turn missing evidence into a claim that a clinic has no website. Personalization must come from fresh, attributable lead evidence.

## Follow-up intent

After three days, briefly check whether the clinic wants to discuss the proposal. Stop on a reply, opt-out, bounce, wrong-person response or booked meeting. Existing sending windows and limits can delay the follow-up further.

The first controlled release expires within 24 hours. It therefore cannot authorize a day-three follow-up: review and renew its scope before that step becomes eligible. Do not shorten the campaign delay or extend the gate automatically.

The campaign requests AI generation, but does not enable AI or change its zero allowance. The example above is manually drafted. Real messages require existing plan generation, validation and approval. No database record, lead verification or approval is created by this document.
